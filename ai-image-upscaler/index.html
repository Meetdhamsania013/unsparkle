How to upscale an image to 4K

  1. Drop your imageDrop a photo, AI image or illustration on the box above.
  2. Pick Photo or Art and the sizeChoose 📷 Photo for real photos or 🎨 Art / Anime for drawings, then HD 2× or 4K. You will see how long it will take before it starts.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI upscaler free?

Yes, free with no account, no watermark and no limits.

Which AI is used?

Real-ESRGAN, a well-known open-source upscaler, with a model for photos and one for art and anime.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Upscaling happens in your browser and your images stay on your device.

How long does 4K take?

From a few seconds with a graphics card to a few minutes on a slow computer. The time is shown before you start and you can cancel at any moment.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, with a size limit that fits the phone’s memory so the page does not crash.
